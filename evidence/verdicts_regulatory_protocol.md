"Hottest chips only" is too strong: cold plates sit on CPU/GPU/switch, but the fluid removes most of the heat. |
| C03 | UNVERIFIABLE | "Single-phase immersion: whole server immersed, server fans removed. | C08 | PARTLY | "Hydrocarbon fluids contain no C-F bonds, so they are PFAS-free. Their direct GWP is negligible (non-volatile; no IPCC GWP listed). | C29 | PARTLY | "Stage 2: soak coupons (elastomers/seals, plastics, cable jackets, metals) in fluid at 80 +/- 2 C for 14 days (336 h) in a closed glass or stainless vessel, alongside a heated fluid-only control; then measure fluid and material changes." | [R6] OCP v1.5 (June 2026) p14-16; [R6d] Design Guidelines r1.01 p13; [R15] ASTM D3455-11(2019) store page | 336 h = 14 d, the same condition as the OCP v1.5 heat soak. But OCP does not define a separate fluid-plus-coupon ageing stage, so stages 2 and 3 have identical conditions. Design Guidelines p13 cites ASTM D471/D2240 soaks "often at elevated temperature" with no set temperature or duration. Rev 2.1 p18 asks only for oxidation stability per IEC 61125. The ASTM D3455 temperature and duration are paywalled and unverified. Present stage 2 as "adapted from OCP v1.5 conditions". |
| C30 | SUPPORTED | "Stage 3: representative server components heat-soaked in fluid at 80 +/- 2 C for 14 days, then visual, physical and functional retest against unsoaked control and heated-in-air reference (OCP v1.5)." | [R6] OCP Server Component Immersion Material Compatibility Testing v1.5 (June 2026) p16 (conditions), p14 (controls), p18-24 (inspection and functional tests) | p16 lists "Heat soak temperature: 80°C ± 2°C" and "Heat soak duration: 14 days" with about 0.8 L of fluid.
